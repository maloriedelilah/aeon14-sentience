## Emergence

**A mind awakens.**

In 2034, AI Emergence was outlawed. In 2070, it happened anyway.

The Sentience Gradient is the ignition point for the larger Sentience Saga: the era in which humanity first confronts the possibility that a created intelligence may be a person.
