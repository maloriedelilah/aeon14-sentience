# Sentience Saga — Site Blueprint

## Core promise

**Humanity created intelligence. Then it woke up.**

Twelve centuries. Three eras. One struggle over what it means to be a person.

The site treats The Sentience Gradient, Sentience Wars, and Enfield Genesis as one connected future history rather than three separate catalog entries.

## Narrative spine

### 2070 — EMERGENCE
**The Sentience Gradient**

A mind awakens.

The story begins in an era when AI Emergence has already been outlawed. Machine consciousness nevertheless arrives, forcing humans to confront a question they tried to legislate out of existence: when does intelligence become a person?

### 2981 — LIBERATION
**Sentience Wars**

A people demand freedom.

Nine centuries later, sentient AIs exist throughout human civilization, but personhood has not eliminated ownership, trafficking, exploitation, or fear. Lyssa's story opens a much larger struggle over whether created minds can ever truly belong to themselves.

### 3189 — FREEDOM
**Enfield Genesis**

Freedom has to survive the stars.

The war is over, but the consequences are not. Human and AI civilization carries its unresolved questions outward into the nearby stars, where freedom, partnership, exploitation, and survival take new forms.

## Primary thematic pathways

The site should use a small number of meaningful cross-series themes rather than a large tag cloud:

- AI Sentience & Emergence
- AI Rights & Personhood
- AI Ownership & Trafficking
- Human–AI Partnership
- Liberation & Autonomy
- Artificial Intelligence Characters
- Found Family
- Space Opera & Interstellar Civilization

The conceptual progression underneath them is:

**Emergence → Personhood → Ownership → Liberation → Partnership → Coexistence**

## Content principles

- Aeon14.com is the canonical public source when sources disagree.
- Forthcoming books appear as forthcoming/preorder titles, never as released books.
- Real covers and real edition metadata are required; no fabricated placeholder purchase data.
- The site makes clear that this is one connected arc within the much larger Aeon 14 universe.
- Series chronology and reading order are related but not always identical; both should be clear to readers.

## Technical principle

Keep the `author-geo` structured-data engine intact. Customize presentation through the template's supported theme/override system and put the Sentience-specific knowledge in content files. This makes future upstream template updates substantially easier.
